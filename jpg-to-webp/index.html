❓ FAQ: JPG to WebP Insights

Why should I convert JPG to WebP?

WebP is a modern image format that provides superior compression. Converting to WebP helps you fix the "Serve images in next-gen formats" error in Google PageSpeed, leading to faster load times and better SEO rankings.

What is the difference between Best and Optimized quality?

Best Quality (88%) preserves nearly all original detail, while Optimized (75%) finds the "sweet spot" where file size is tiny but the image still looks sharp to the human eye.

Is this converter safe for sensitive photos?

Yes. Because all processing happens locally in your browser, your images are never uploaded to a cloud. You can even use this tool while offline once the page is loaded.
