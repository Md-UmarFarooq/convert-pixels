❓ FAQ: PNG to JPG Conversion

Will JPG files be smaller than PNG?

Yes! JPG compression typically reduces PNG file sizes by 80-95%. A 1MB PNG becomes just 50-200KB as JPG while maintaining great quality.

Is there any quality loss in PNG to JPG conversion?

JPG uses efficient compression that discards some data imperceptible to the human eye. At Maximum Quality setting, the visual difference is negligible while file size drops dramatically.

Why can't I upload more than 20 files?

To ensure maximum speed and prevent browser crashes on mobile, we've optimized the batch limit to 20 files per session.
