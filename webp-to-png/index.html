❓ FAQ: WebP to PNG Conversion

Will I lose transparency converting WebP to PNG?

No. Our converter preserves alpha transparency from WebP to PNG. Transparent backgrounds stay transparent in the output PNG files.

What is the 256-color (Optimized) option?

It's a smart optimization that reduces PNG file size by limiting the palette to 256 colors. It keeps images looking sharp while minimizing the size increase from WebP.

Why convert WebP to PNG if WebP is smaller?

For compatibility reasons. Some editing software, social media platforms, and older systems work better with PNG format. Our tool makes conversion seamless.
